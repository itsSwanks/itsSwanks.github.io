Track Your Pep

Help and answers for tracking your peptide protocol — logging doses, building schedules, reminders, and progress.

Getting started

  1. Add a peptide. Open the Peptides tab and tap +. Give it a name, dose, unit, and route, then pick a color and icon.
  2. Build a schedule. Choose daily, weekly, every-N-days, or several times a day — and turn on reminders.
  3. Log your doses. On the Today tab, tap Log dose (or mark it skipped). You can also add an as-needed log anytime.
  4. Track progress. The Progress tab shows weight & BMI, adherence, streaks, and wellbeing over time.

Frequently asked questions

How do reminders work?

TYP schedules notifications for your upcoming doses, and you can log a dose right from the notification. Make sure notifications are allowed for TYP in the iOS Settings app.

Can I use TYP on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on each device and your peptides, schedules, and history stay in sync.

Do widgets and Live Activities work?

Yes. Add the next-dose, today, or full-schedule widgets to your Home or Lock Screen, and your next dose can appear as a Live Activity so it's always a glance away.

How do I export or back up my data?

Go to Settings → Backup. You can export a PDF report or a CSV, or create an encrypted backup that you can restore later.

Is my data private?

Your records are stored in your own account, isolated from other users, and encrypted in transit and at rest. There are no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs. Full details are shown on the Privacy screen inside the app.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every record associated with it. It can't be undone.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link. You can also sign in with Apple instead.
