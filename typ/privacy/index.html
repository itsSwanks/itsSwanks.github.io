Privacy Policy

TYP · Track Your Pep

Last updated: 19 July 2026

This policy explains what information TYP (“the app”, “we”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. TYP is a personal record-keeping and reminder tool for tracking information you choose to enter.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you use to create an account (or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple, which may be a private relay address).
  • The data you enter: the peptides, doses, schedules, and logs you record, plus any weight, body-metric, wellbeing (mood, energy, appetite, sleep), side-effect, and note entries you add.
  • Basic technical data needed to sync and secure your account (e.g. timestamps for last-write-wins syncing).

We do not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

How your data is stored and protected

Your records are stored in your own account in our cloud backend (Supabase). Each user’s rows are isolated from other users with Row Level Security, and data is protected by encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Your data is not end-to-end encrypted.

Because it is not end-to-end encrypted, TYP’s operators can technically access your data and may review it where necessary to operate and protect the service — for example to investigate abuse or misuse, resolve a support issue, or comply with the law. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

How we use your data

  • To provide the app’s core features — storing your entries, syncing across your devices, and sending the reminders you enable.
  • To secure your account and keep the service running safely.
  • To respond to you if you contact support.

Reminders & notifications

If you enable reminders, TYP schedules local notifications on your device for your upcoming doses. You can turn these off at any time in the app or in your device’s Settings.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app. You can also export your data (PDF or CSV, or an encrypted backup) before deleting.

Children

TYP is not directed to children and is intended for adults. Do not use the app if you are under the age required to consent to processing of your data where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at support@peptrack.app.
